Idle Cart Merchant

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Idle Cart Merchant ("the game") is a free game for Android. This page explains what information is involved when you play and why. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. Ads and purchases are handled by Google, under Google's own privacy terms.

Your game progress

Your progress (coins, ventures, settings and so on) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to share personal information with other players.

Ads

The game is free and shows optional ads (for example, watching an ad for a temporary profit boost). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Optional in-game purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google tells the game only which item you bought, so it can give it to you. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
